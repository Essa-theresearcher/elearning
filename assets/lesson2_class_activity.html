Class Activity: How the Internet Actually Works

This is the hands-on portion you'd normally do live in class, adapted so you can work through it on your own. Take your time — nothing here is timed or graded.

Six short activities · ~25–30 minutes total · A terminal and this week's recording nearby is useful here

1HTTP Status Sleuth

Read the scenario, guess the status code, then reveal it.

Scenario: You call an API for a product page that was deleted last month. The server responds fine — it just has nothing at that address anymore.
404 Not Found The server itself is working fine — it responded — there's just nothing left at that specific path. That's exactly what separates a 404 from a 500, where the server itself fails.

2TCP or UDP?

Predict which protocol fits, and why, before revealing.

Scenario: You're building a live video call feature. A dropped frame here and there is fine — nobody wants the call to freeze for a second just to guarantee every frame arrives.
UDP Speed matters more than a perfect copy here. TCP's confirm-and-resend overhead would make the call feel laggy — better to drop an occasional frame than freeze waiting for it to be resent.

3Field Notes: Trace Your Own Route

A real-world task using your own machine — no reveal, just observation.

Terminal command: macOS/Linux: traceroute google.com Windows: tracert google.com
